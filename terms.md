## 4. The free app and Plus

Every household starts on a **free 14-day trial of Plus**, from the day the household is created.
No payment details are needed for the trial. When it ends, the household moves to the free plan
unless someone has bought Plus.

**Free plan:** everything in the app, with up to **10 active chores** per household.

**Plus:** no limit on the number of chores. Plus covers the whole household: one member pays and
everyone in the household gets it.

If a household goes back to the free plan with more than 10 chores, nothing is deleted: every chore
keeps working and can be edited, done or removed. The household cannot add another until it is
below the limit.
